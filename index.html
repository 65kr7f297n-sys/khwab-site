The Dream

A tranquil retreat
on the Pacific coast

Welcome to Khwab (meaning dream), a 2-bedroom, 2-bath coastal haven nestled at the north end of The Sea Ranch. This is a place where spectacular sunsets unfold slowly beyond meadows dotted with grazing deer, where jackrabbits lope across the morning grass and sleek red fox catch your eye from the deck.

Every detail of this home has been curated for comfort and ease: smart lock entry, fiber-optic internet, a secluded hot tub under a canopy of stars, and a kitchen equipped for everything from a sunrise pour-over to a full feast after a day on the bluff trail.

This is not a rental. This is a restoration.

Why Khwab

What makes this place
unforgettable

🌊

Ocean views from the main rooms

The living room, front deck, and master bedroom all face west across open meadows toward the Pacific. Every evening is a private sunset show. No two the same.

♨️

Private hot tub under the stars

A wind-protected, secluded hot tub on the back deck awaits after your adventures. The Sea Ranch's dark skies make for extraordinary stargazing.

🦌

Wildlife at your doorstep

Deer graze the meadow each morning. Red fox slink along the treeline. Jackrabbits bound past the front deck. Great blue heron meditate in the meadow grass, pelicans glide along the bluffs, and sea lions sun themselves on the rocks below. Bobcats pass through on occasion, too.
